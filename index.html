Our approach

Only real reviews.

A review only helps when it is real. Uplein is built around that.

  • No fake reviews, ever.
  • Everyone gets the same request. We never pick by mood.
  • No rewards or discounts for reviews.
  • A click is not a review. We count one only when you confirm it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Uplein write reviews for us?

No. Your customers write them, in their own words.

Which review sites are supported?

Google first, with your review link and a QR code. More may follow.

Do you send text messages?

Email comes first. Texts follow once approved for your business, which takes some time.

Is there a free trial?

We start with a short demo and a paid pilot for early customers.

Can we stop a campaign?

Yes, any time. Customers can also unsubscribe in one click.
