Our approach

Authentic reviews only.

A review is only worth something when it is real. Uplein is built around that, and it also keeps your Google profile safe.

  • No fake reviews, ever.
  • Every customer gets the same request. We never filter by how happy they are.
  • No rewards or discounts in exchange for reviews.
  • A click is a click. We never claim a review was posted unless you tell us it was.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Uplein write reviews for us?

No. Customers write their own reviews, in their own words, on your public review page.

Which review sites are supported?

We start with Google reviews, through your review link and a QR code. More sites may follow.

Do you send text messages?

Email comes first. Text messages follow once sender registration is approved for your business, which takes some time.

Is there a free trial?

We start with a short demo and a paid pilot for early customers. Request a demo and we will walk you through it.

Can we stop a campaign?

Yes. You can pause or stop a campaign at any time, and customers can unsubscribe with one click.
