How it works

From the first call to the first request.

1Create your accountYou create an account and enter your business address and Google review link in Settings.
2We switch on sendingAfter a quick check of your business, we switch sending on.
3Add your customersType one in from your phone in about ten seconds, or import a spreadsheet. Only people who agreed to hear from you get an email. No list? Print your QR code and customers scan it themselves.
4Send and follow upSend a friendly request with one button. One gentle reminder is available. See who got it and who opened the link, and mark the reviews you receive.

Prefer to talk first? A short call is optional: we show you the app in about 15 minutes and answer your questions.

Our rules

Three rules we keep.

  • No fake reviews, and no rewards for reviews.
  • Everyone gets the same request. We never pick by mood.
  • A click is not a review. We count one only when you confirm it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Does Uplein write reviews for us?

No. Your customers write them, in their own words.

Do I have to call you?

No. You can set everything up yourself. A short call is optional, and early customers get personal help.

What do I need to start?

Your business address, the link to your Google review page, and customers who agreed to hear from you.

How do I add customers?

Type one in from your phone, import a spreadsheet, or print your QR code so customers scan it themselves.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They get an email with a button to your Google review page, or scan your QR code.

Which review sites are supported?

Google first, with your review link and a QR code. More may follow.

Do you send text messages?

Not yet. Email comes first, and texts will follow once approved for your business, which takes some time.

Is there a free trial?

No. We start with a short demo and a paid pilot for early customers. See the Paid pilot section for the details.

Can we stop a campaign?

Yes, any time. Customers can also unsubscribe in one click.
